Asexuality: Details First

What this is

I keep having to explain the basics of asexuality to folks and figured it would be helpful to just have a dictionary of terms and details that one could then use to get a quick idea of what asexuality actually is.

The hope is that concepts explained clearly will make things easier to understand, and with that clear foundation you can then build up to bigger concepts.

I think in this manner because I'm Autistic, this is meant to target that style of thought, but isn't exclusive to us, hopefully it's sensible too to non-autistic and neurotypical folks.

The raw basics

  • Attraction: This is when you experience an emotional draw to another person over some characteristic they exhibit. There's many different types of attraction. The idea is that based off of some other real-life person's characteristics, you are drawn to other people over a desire to experience them in XYZ manner. You can feel this either immediately or as you get to know them.
  • Sexual attraction: An attraction type. The XYZ here is sex. You are experiencing attraction to the other person and you want to experience them further through having sex.
  • Romance: a structured, formalized and ritualized relationship with someone to meet societal expectations of a relationship. This is not love in itself. Several of the rituals involve dating, marriage, taking on a spouse's name, vows of commitment, etc.
  • Romantic attraction: An attraction type. The XYZ here is romance. You are experiencing attraction to the other person and you want to experience them further through romance.
  • Asexuality: You do not experience sexual attraction, or you only experience it very little, or you only experience it through highly specific, atypical circumstances.
  • Aromanticism: You do not experience romantic attraction, or you only experience it very little, or you only experience it through highly specific, atypical circumstances.
  • Aroace: You experience both asexuality and aromanticism.
